# Qualification phase proofs — software readiness, not a new trial

This amendment keeps the corrected worker, profile, mechanics and physical
acceptance unchanged. It does not authorize physics, a new clock, optimization,
a candidate, or continuation of any closed attempt. The historical runner stays
closed. A future exact admission must bind the reviewed source, worker, inputs,
private inventory and scoped storage allowance.

| Phase | Work | Normal integration envelope |

There is **one shared 5,020-integration reserve**, giving 117,080 maximum, not a
new reserve for each phase. Every native call still reserves 5,020 integrations.
All source decisions, including parity duplicates and initialization, count
against 1,024; WEAK forecasts are separately counted. The 147,000 candidate
reserve remains untouched. Physics stops at T+2,100 seconds and all work at
T+3,600. Backup/restore waits spend this same clock. Each intermediate proof has
a 180-second allowance; no failed or uncertain external operation is retried.

## Replacement-aware serialized-state guard

This is an **output/serialized-state** bound, not a transient WASM heap/RSS bound.
Before every native dispatch, admission includes:

```
retained active files
+ terminal output not already represented (untouched learner, replay, cursor)
+ exact checkpoints of every unchanged tracked runtime
+ bounded REPLACEMENT checkpoint of the advanced runtime
+ pending checkpoint writes and bounded next evidence record
```

The advanced runtime's previous checkpoint is not added twice unless an actual
retained file still contains it. Retained files, comparison runtimes, parity
checkpoints and hold checkpoints always remain additive until an authorized
verified cache lifecycle transition occurs.

The replacement checkpoint contains the exact immutable model, a complete
6 MiB world bound, and the maximum of retained history plus bounded mutation or
a fresh reference/controller. Both alternatives use the same two JSON-string
escaping layers: a null correction head still activates schema 4. The pure
reader does not restore or step a runtime and rejects unaligned source epochs.

The frozen cardinality derivation remains: 25 body slots, 19 body primitives,
floor, striker and five walls, at most 325 primitive pairs, 27 DoF. Two manifold
copies with 16 contacts per pair, 256 bytes per tracked/solver point and 1,024
bytes metadata give 3,328,000 bytes; another 2 MiB covers fixed matrices and
world/arena/graph structures, rounded to 6 MiB. A currently larger world is
rejected rather than silently bounded downward.

The fresh-reference component is 12 MiB: at most 250 teacher frames, 501
reference frames and the initial snapshot, through both escaping layers.
One aligned native call adds at most one StudentDecision: 485 numeric values,
fixed IDs and native counter bound to less than 24 KiB. The separate 2 MiB
mutable allowance covers the correction head (21,525 numeric values, at most
688,800 characters at the conservative 32-character width), changed observation,
reference/residual/status scalars, first-control/terminal binding and other fixed
simulation fields. Another 16 KiB covers root/envelope sidecars. These categories
are source-bound; they are not averages inferred from a favorable trajectory.

The pre-call checkpoint is decoded without constructing a runtime. Certification
requires the pinned runtime profile (1.25 ms, 20 substeps, repeat-cycle token),
unchanged model/profile/generation lineage, a started, aligned source with a
reference, explicit completion boolean, no known failure and an empty lifecycle
queue. A normal finite, native-bound pending correction is not a lifecycle reset.
The source-bound next-call branches are:

| Pre-call state | Serialized alternative |
|---|---|
| Incomplete, stable counter + 20 < 800 | Retained runtime + 24 KiB aligned decision + existing mutation/wrapper allowances |
| Incomplete, possibly crossing 800 | Maximum of that branch and retained runtime + 64 KiB empty controller + mutation/wrapper allowances |
| Completed, motion Upright or Reacting | Same bounded empty-controller alternative |
| Unstarted, absent reference, completed Falling/Fallen/Recovering/Halted, or uncertified lineage | Full retained-or-fresh-reference fallback |
| Queued lifecycle command, disposed runtime, invalid schema/alignment/lineage | No dispatch |

Why the extra branches are safe: first success resets both motion timers and
sets Upright. A completed Upright/Reacting-to-Falling transition resets the
settling timer. At most 20 × 1.25 ms cannot satisfy the unchanged 0.3-second
Fallen threshold. The controller can be replaced once with an empty controller,
but cannot create a new reference in that call. Completed Falling with an old
settling timer is a counterexample and keeps the full fallback. Forecasts never
call the real observer. Source anchors are `motion.rs` fall/settling transitions,
`controller.rs` reference creation, rearm and first-success blocks; their exact
hashes are frozen in the plan. No native/controller code is changed.

The empty-controller bound is explicit: 33 controller/status fields, 42 f32
slots, bounded phase/refusal strings and fixed overhead give at most 7,780 raw
JSON bytes, or 31,126 bytes after two string-escaping layers. We reserve 64 KiB
**in addition to the whole existing normalized runtime**, without subtracting
or reconstructing old history. The 6 MiB world, 2 MiB mutable and 16 KiB wrapper
allowances stay unchanged. Every actual call still reserves 5,020 integrations.
The compact branch inputs and projection are retained with native call evidence
and terminal capture, including partial/failed calls.

This guard can stop before a complete trajectory. It makes **no universal
32 MiB completion guarantee**. It reserves a complete terminal capture before
allowing another call, including known failed runtimes and uncertain charges.

## Durable phase evidence and exact dependency closure

PARITY and BELLY each produce one immutable private archive, without a duplicate
learner. Their application validation reconstructs physical checkpoints and
mapper/replay chronology but explicitly does **not** claim learner restoration.
The final NEARBY terminal generation uses the existing two-slot adapter and
includes the untouched learner/optimizers/RNG, full diagnostic replay/mapper and
current ledger/runtime. Diagnostic partial strata remain nontrainable; missing
BACK training strata remain visible.

Each reference binds run/source/worker/input identities, private file ID, exact
archive and manifest hashes, size, every member hash/size, ledger begin/end,
phase status and prior reference hashes. Native audit rows conserve the phase's
real/forecast integration totals and contiguous call numbers. Final restoration
redownloads exact dependency bytes, reconstructs the entire raw chronology,
checks global replay indices/raw spans and prior row membership, and restores
fresh application objects. A missing, corrupt, mixed or uncertain dependency
cannot pass. Input references bind the already verified shared archive and the
new metered seed archive; unchanged inputs are never uploaded again.

Only after exact private readback and application validation does the process
persist the phase reference. Only then may it free the transient runtime and
remove **new exact duplicate local working/archive caches**. No old closed proof,
original, remote evidence or uncertain data is removed. A journal-commit/eviction
interruption retains the reference and recognizes the exact already-committed
raw suffix, preventing duplicate replay or a native retry. There is no generic
restart authorization. A later attempt needs a distinct explicit admission.

Hold evidence retains the existing per-sample-derived extrema/stability and
first-800/final checkpoints, not all 48,000 hold native rows. Even a failure to
reserve the final checkpoint copy retains the measured hold summary, explicitly
not passed/hash-bound until the final copy succeeds. The unchanged material-slip
metric is maximum anchored material-point excursion, not cumulative travel.

## Storage and stopping

- Active qualification output/terminal payload: **32 MiB**.
- Each archive: **18 MiB**; two immutable phase proofs plus one terminal slot.
- Remote cumulative exposure: **160 MiB**, including overwritten revisions,
  Trash and uncertain writes; no quota credit from replacement/cache eviction.
- Scoped recovery staging: **192 MiB**, not a whole-workspace/toolchain cap.
  Live output is reported separately in the total recovery footprint.

With the closed inventory at 43,566,444 bytes, the seed envelope 1,962,859 and
three archive envelopes, cumulative admission is **102,152,407 bytes**. The
measured protected-input base gives a single-phase packaging peak of
191,599,370 bytes and a separate total including 32 MiB live output of
225,153,802 bytes. Those are not a universal dependency-closure peak: every
subsequent readback additionally admits its exact archive plus extracted payload
against the measured staging occupancy. The next-dispatch guard reserves this
known dependency cost together with terminal packaging.

An oversized archive is retained locally with prior durable proofs; physics
stops. Packaging can be diagnosed later without rerunning completed work.
Software rehearsals use the production archive/receipt adapter and real
mapper/replay/learner serialization, but scripted physics and transfers. Their
success establishes neither real parity/preservation nor a physical acceptance.

## Preservation-only continuation of evidence, not of a closed run

A separate, explicitly admitted preservation attempt may use a **durable foreign
PARITY proof**. This does not reopen its source attempt, reuse its deadline or
import its execution ledger. The prerequisite must bind the exact original ZIP,
manifest and every member, original run/source/plan/ledger, private readback and
application-restore receipt. A denied or merely local archive is insufficient.
Its original 4,420 integrations and 50 source decisions remain historical facts,
not entries in the new run. No new physical attempt is authorized by this source.

Pure preflight rejects missing, nondurable or incompatible prerequisites before
creating an output, loading a runtime or starting a clock. Compatibility includes
the exact corrected worker/glue, compiled seven-feature build, model, actors,
profile, protocol and all three triggered seed hashes. The foreign archive is
validated under its **original** identity and plan; its 400-sample off/zero and
200-sample restored comparisons are not executed again. Nonstepping operational
restoration occurs inside the new clock. A physical-only restore never constructs
a learner and therefore cannot reset global Python, NumPy or Torch RNG, including
on failure. The foreign cursor, mapper, raw rows, replay and RNG are not imported.

The new process reuses the exact verified seed archive. It restores the untouched
triggered BELLY and nearby BACK seeds, preserving impact/source counters. It does
not call `coldPrepare` or `coldQualify`, upload seeds again, or substitute a new
fixture. Its own dependency chain is **BELLY → terminal**; the historical PARITY
reference is separate. Every final dependency restore verifies both closures,
without treating the foreign ledger or old raw rows as new experience.
